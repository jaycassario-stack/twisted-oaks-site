Venue guide · Shamong, NJ

Bishop Farmstead wedding photography

By Jay Cassario · Updated November 2026

Bishop Farmstead in Shamong, NJ is one of my favorite places to photograph a wedding, and to be honest, it’s a big part of why so many of my couples end up there. There are beautiful portrait spots all across the property, everything can happen in one place from getting ready to the last dance, and the light just works. Here’s what I’ve learned from photographing weddings there, along with four real weddings you can look through.

At a glance

  • Getting ready: a separate building with two rooms, plus an Airstream for a smaller group of groomsmen.
  • Ceremony: an outdoor garden site where the sun lines up just right for ceremony photos.
  • Rain plan: a large tent right outside the barn that converts into the ceremony space.
  • Portraits: gardens, trees, open lawns and the red barn, all within a short walk.
  • Reception: the barn, with string lights and chandeliers.

Getting ready

One thing I really appreciate about Bishop Farmstead is the getting-ready setup. They have a separate building with two rooms, one for each side, so everyone stays close and nobody is a drive or a long walk away. That makes a big difference for me, because I can move back and forth and catch the quiet moments on both sides without missing anything. They also have an Airstream that’s perfect for a smaller group of groomsmen, and it makes for some really nice, relaxed prep photos.

The ceremony

The outdoor ceremony spot is positioned perfectly, so the sun lines up just right for gorgeous ceremony photos. Couples walk down a grass aisle lined with wooden chairs to a pergola that’s usually covered in flowers, with the gardens all around, and it looks a little different every season, from summer greens to the reds and golds of a November afternoon.

If it rains

Rain is always the question, so here’s the honest answer. Bishop Farmstead has a large tent right outside the barn that can easily be turned into the ceremony space. It’s close to everything, so the day still flows the same way, and the light under a tent can actually be really soft and flattering.

Portraits around the property

This is the part I love most. There are beautiful portrait locations all across the property, which is one of the reasons I love shooting there… the gardens and paths, big shade trees, open lawns, a red Japanese maple that turns bright red in the fall, and the red barn, which is great for the wedding party. Because everything is close, we can move between spots quickly and spend more time making photos and less time walking. For Meg and Mike, we even finished with a few portraits by an old wagon lit with candles just after sunset.

The barn reception

Once the sun goes down, the barn takes over. Between the wood beams, the string lights and the chandeliers, it’s warm and full of character, and the dance floor sits right in the middle of everything, so the party never feels far away. Long story short, it’s a great room for a celebration.
