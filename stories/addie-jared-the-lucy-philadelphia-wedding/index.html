Addie & Jared

Getting Ready
Loews Philadelphia Hotel
Ceremony
St. John the Evangelist Church, Center City
Portraits
Old City & City Hall
Reception
The Lucy by Cescaphe, Philadelphia

It was cold, it was January and it was Philadelphia... which, to be honest, is just about my favorite way to see the city. Addie and Jared both got ready at the Loews on Market Street, the girls in matching navy pajamas and Addie in white feathers, while a certain French bulldog made the rounds between both suites. Before the ceremony they traded letters, and Addie wore a few pieces that meant a lot to her: her mother’s earrings, her sister-in-law’s veil and her great-grandmother’s purse.

They were married at St. John the Evangelist in Center City, and that white altar is something you have to see in person. After the ceremony the two of them ducked into a corner bar for a quick drink, and then we spent the afternoon around Old City with the wedding party, faux fur wraps and all. Once the sun went down we grabbed a few more on the city streets, with City Hall glowing behind them.

And the reception? The Lucy was packed from start to finish. Their first dance ended under a shower of rose petals, there was a Penn State cake, and the dance floor never really emptied. We wrapped the night with a sparkler send-off out into the cold.

In their words

“Twisted Oaks was everything we could have dreamed of and so much more! They were professional and responsive from the very beginning… Their editing style gave us the moody, old hollywood look we were going for.”Addie, on The Knot
